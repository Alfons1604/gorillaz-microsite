Biography

Gorillaz was created in 1998 in London by musician Damon Albarn and artist Jamie Hewlett. Instead of posing for photos, the band exists as four animated characters: 2D (vocals and keyboards), Murdoc Niccals (bass), Noodle (guitar) and Russel Hobbs (drums).

Their self-titled debut album arrived in 2001 and included “Clint Eastwood.” Demon Days (2005) and the hit “Feel Good Inc.” turned them into global stars, and in 2006 Guinness World Records named them the most successful virtual band.

Collaboration is part of the band’s identity. Over the years, Gorillaz have recorded with artists from many different worlds, including De La Soul, Snoop Dogg, Lou Reed, Tame Impala and Bad Bunny.

Latest album

The Mountain

2026 · 15 songs · 66 min

Gorillaz’s ninth studio album, released on 27 February 2026 on their own label, KONG. Built around grief, death and the afterlife, it was recorded in India after Damon Albarn and Jamie Hewlett lost their parents within days of each other.

Western pop and electronics meet sitar, tabla and bansuri. Guests include Bizarrap, Trueno, Johnny Marr, Sparks, IDLES and Anoushka Shankar, plus posthumous appearances by Dennis Hopper and Bobby Womack.

Debuted at number one on the UK albums chart.
